About

The person behind the projects.

I'm nineteen, studying Computer Science at National University of Lesotho, and a freelance developer.I design and build digital products with a focus on turning ideas into practical,purposeful solutions.

My interest in technology started with web development and grew into a broader interest in software engineering and artificial intelligence. Since then, I’ve worked on projects ranging from business websites to platforms designed around real-world problems.

Freelancing has given me the opportunity to take ideas from a conversation to something people can actually use. It has also taught me that good software is not just about writing code, it is about understanding the problem, making deliberate decisions, and building something that works.

I’m currently focused on deepening my understanding of computer science and developing the skills to build increasingly ambitious software. There is a lot more I want to create, and this is only the beginning.
